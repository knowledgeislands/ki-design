# GDR-KI-DESIGN-009: The Observatory as the local app's mark

## Context

With the Tower dropped (GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-011), the local app needed a mark that is not combative. The Observatory already means insight and analytics in the canon, and the local app's working name is the Observatory.

## Decision

**The domed Observatory is the local app's mark, whatever the app is finally called.** The Beacon is the Observatory's lamp: a lantern with a flame, distinct from the Lighthouse.

## Consequences

- The `observatory` glyph and symbol serve as the app's mark; `beacon` is the Beacon's tab glyph.
- The compass rose and the Observatory dome never stand in for the Knowledge Islands logo, which is the canon roundel.
- The app's product name is still open ([OPEN.md](OPEN.md)); the mark does not change with it.
