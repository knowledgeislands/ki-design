# GDR-KI-DESIGN-033: Ten instruments under the four jobs, by convergence

## Context

The Observatory design groups the app's fifteen tabs into ten instruments under the four jobs, Observe · Attend · Act · Extend ([GDR-KI-DESIGN-016](GDR-KI-DESIGN-016-the-four-jobs.md)). Kit's approach to the overhaul is to apply the design to the app as it is without losing anything, then converge. On 19 October 2026 Kit accepted the recommendation.

## Decision

**The ten-instrument grouping is adopted, by convergence:** the design is applied to the app as it is first, then the instruments are consolidated one at a time.

## Consequences

- Today's instruments stay until each consolidation lands.
- The Observatory's own records own the instruments and the order of consolidation; this record states the design target.
