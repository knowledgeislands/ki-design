# GDR-KI-DESIGN-015: Repositories are islands; territories are archipelagos

## Context

An earlier draft made the archipelago a non-governing grouping of islands by character, crossing territories. That needed two grouping lines on one chart. On 18 October 2026 it was decided that a territory is drawn as an archipelago.

In `ki-principal`, SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-005 (11 October 2026) still describes an archipelago as a grouping independent of jurisdiction, which a territory may span. Under GDR-KI-DESIGN-002 this agreed item wins over the official text until it is enacted.

## Decision

**A repository is an island. A territory is an archipelago:** its islands in one sea region with a tinted border. An island may sit apart from its territory's region as an exclave. The earlier reading is withdrawn, so a chart never draws a second grouping line across territories.

## Consequences

- The Known Lands chart, the scope picker and the symbol rows draw territories as archipelagos.
- The status is _agreed_: the change is owed to `ki-principal`, where SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-005 needs updating. It is listed in [OPEN.md](OPEN.md) so it is not forgotten.
