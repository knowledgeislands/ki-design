# GDR-KI-DESIGN-028: Observatory stays the local app's name until Gate B

## Context

Kit has said the local app will act, not only observe, so "Observatory" may be the wrong name. The Tower is no longer a candidate, and the app's mark is the Observatory whatever it is called ([GDR-KI-DESIGN-009](GDR-KI-DESIGN-009-the-observatory-mark.md)). On 19 October 2026 Kit accepted the recommendation not to rename it now.

## Decision

**The local app is called the Observatory until Gate B.** Its mark is the domed Observatory either way.

## Revisit when

The Observatory reaches Gate B, when there is evidence of how the app is used to act as well as observe. The name is decided then, with that evidence; the mark does not change with it.

## Consequences

- Copy, boards and briefs write "Observatory".
- A rename after Gate B is a copy change in the apps and the site, not a change to the mark or the glyph.
