# GDR-KI-DESIGN-029: Steward roles, following GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-014

## Context

The design drew workers on the map by role: Steward, Archivist, Cartographer, Builder, Navigator, Scout, Historian and Guardian, all proposed. The question was whether to write them into `Philosophy/Model/Agents` or map them onto the roles the model already describes before the Field Guide publishes them. On 19 October 2026 Kit accepted the recommendation to map first and add only what is missing, and `ki-principal` enacted it as GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-014.

## Decision

**Seven steward roles are official: Steward, Archivist, Cartographer, Builder, Navigator, Scout and Historian. The Guardian is not adopted; boundary-keeping is drawn as the Steward at the Council Hall.**

- A role is a hat, not a species: any agent, human or AI, may wear one, and a role grants no authority.
- The explainer boards' names read as these roles: Curator as the Archivist, Gardener as the Steward, Harbourman as the Navigator, Observer as the Historian, and Governance as the Steward.

## Consequences

- The seven role symbols are official; `guardian` is retired in `retiredSymbols`, and the `role-guardian` glyph stays, mapped to no symbol.
- Public copy no longer marks the seven roles proposed.
