# GDR-KI-DESIGN-030: The lore sheet, following GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-015

## Context

Illustrations and copy reach for the same world, but nothing named which motifs belong to it, so drafts drift into fantasy. The design work recommended one page of named motifs for the Aesthetics pillar, and deciding Whale song with it, since it is a creature. On 19 October 2026 Kit accepted, and `ki-principal` enacted the Lore Sheet as GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-015.

## Decision

**Art and copy draw only on motifs the Lore Sheet or the Symbol Library names:** the Great Library, the Capital, the Port and its customs house, the Mill, the tides as the cycle, the Known Lands and their fog, the compass, and the Ruins as honoured retirement. **Creatures, magic and crystals are ruled out, and Whale song with them.**

## Consequences

- The `whale-song` signal is retired in `retiredSymbols`; no art draws a whale.
- The never-draw list in docs/spec/illustration.md names creatures without exception.
- A new motif needs a later decision in `ki-principal` first.
