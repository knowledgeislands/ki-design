# GDR-KI-DESIGN-014: The painting as the home hero

## Context

The site needs one image that makes the world legible at first sight. The painted Arcadia, variation 1, was chosen on 18 October 2026, and Kit wants the original kept. The Ancient tile set that would let the chart itself be painted does not exist yet.

## Decision

**"The painting is the legend; the chart is the map."** The painting leads: it is the home hero and the `/arcadia/` hero. The engraved Modern chart follows it: the Arcadia section on the home page, the chart on `/arcadia/`, diagrams and the local app.

## Consequences

- The painting has been live as the home hero since site commit `07f4c02`, Kit's call on 18 October 2026.
- A chart / painting switch comes later, once the Ancient tiles exist.
- The painting is generated reference art. It is treated as unlicensed until the art-licensing decision is recorded, and the site says so.
- Repaints keep variation 1's composition, light and palette; see the handoff.
