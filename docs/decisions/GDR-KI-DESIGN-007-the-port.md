# GDR-KI-DESIGN-007: The Port

## Context

The canon had a Harbour as the integration point and used "Port" for operational systems. Kit decided on 18 October 2026 that the Port replaces the Harbour: a port has more modes of transport than a harbour, from docks to airports, rail depots and border crossings. The same change separated guidance from governance, which the canon had given to one symbol. Knowledge Islands Principal enacted it as GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-010, _The Port_.

## Decision

**This repository draws the Port as GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-010 defines it.**

- The Port is entry and exchange, in any mode. Its four modes are drawn as districts of one Port: dock (material carried in by hand), rail depot (scheduled feeds), airport (long-range arrivals, drawn as a tall mooring mast with a moored airship, never jets or runways) and customs house (a governed boundary where things are checked).
- The Harbour is retired. It is never written or drawn, and its id is never reused.
- The Lighthouse means guidance only. The Council Hall means governance.
- Operational systems move to the Mill, official since GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-013.

## Consequences

- The symbol library marks the Port, its four modes, the Lighthouse and the Council Hall official, and lists `harbour` as retired.
- The icon set has `port`, `dock`, `rail-depot`, `airport` and `customs-house`; the tile contract has a structure for each.
- Site copy reads "The Port — Capture".
