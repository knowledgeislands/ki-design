# GDR-KI-DESIGN-021: Civic towers in the architectural families

## Context

The architectural families give each archetype its materials and silhouette. The Collegiate family listed towers and the Civic harbour town listed clock towers, while the non-combative principle (GDR-KI-DESIGN-008) rules out citadels, towers, keeps and battlements. The choice was to keep civic towers as architecture or drop them from the families. On 19 October 2026 Kit chose to allow them and confirmed it explicitly; `ki-principal` amended GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-011 to match.

## Decision

**Civic towers are allowed in the architectural families, as architecture only: never fortified and never on the Capital.**

- Allowed: a clock tower, which also draws the Calendar zone, and cloister or college towers in the Collegiate family.
- A civic tower has no battlements, arrow slits, machicolations, curtain walls or gatehouse, and it never stands on the Capital's flower.
- A civic tower is not a symbol. The retired `tower` symbol and glyph stay retired; the symbol language stays non-combative.

## Consequences

- docs/spec/tiles-and-buildings.md and the building standards record the conditions.
- The non-combative principle reads: no citadels, keeps, battlements or fortified towers, and no walls around the Capital.
