# GDR-KI-DESIGN-027: No curtain wall for now

## Context

Kit once liked the idea of a low wall with open gates around the knowledge base in a territory's Principal repository: the line between settled knowledge and work in motion, with the Enactment Process as its gate. It was not adopted with the Capital ([GDR-KI-DESIGN-008](GDR-KI-DESIGN-008-the-capital-and-the-non-combative-principle.md)), because a wall reads as a fortification. On 19 October 2026 Kit accepted the recommendation to revisit it only in a form that does not.

## Decision

**No curtain wall is drawn. The Capital stays open.**

## Revisit when

A form is proposed that marks the line between settled knowledge and work in motion without reading as a fortification: for example a hedge, a low kerb, a change of paving or a colonnade, with the Enactment Process as its opening. Such a proposal is judged against the non-combative principle and needs a meaning in `ki-principal` first.

## Consequences

- No tile set, painting or chart draws a wall around a knowledge base or the Capital.
- The settled / in-motion line is shown by the zones themselves: the Library in the Capital, the Streams as rivers.
