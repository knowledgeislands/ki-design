# GDR-KI-DESIGN-026: The roundel is the logo

## Context

The canon logo is a roundel, an island and an arch (`ki-principal/Pillars/Aesthetics/Logo/logo.png`), while the site header and mood boards used a compass rose. On 19 October 2026 Kit accepted the recommendation.

## Decision

**The canon roundel is the Knowledge Islands logo. The compass rose is the Realm's orientation motif and the candidate favicon. The local app's mark is the Observatory.**

## Consequences

- The site header uses the roundel; the compass may appear as an orientation mark on charts and posters, never in place of the logo.
- The roundel is used from the canon file, never redrawn or restyled.
- Whether the favicon is the compass is settled when the site's favicon is drawn; until then it is the candidate.
- The compass is also a lore motif (GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-015).
