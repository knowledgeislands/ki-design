# GDR-KI-DESIGN-016: The local app's four jobs

## Context

The local app is about people's interaction with the knowledge and the work; agents are workers in it. It needed a top-level division that shows the state of the knowledge and the work first, and keeps every change deliberate. DESIGN.md listed the four jobs among its decided items.

## Decision

**The local app has four jobs: Observe · Attend · Act · Extend.**

- **Observe**: the read-only instruments.
- **Attend**: noticing, through the Beacon and "Waiting for you".
- **Act**: the mutating job. It is visibly distinct, and every change goes through plan → confirm → receipt.
- **Extend**: hosting core and island extensions.

## Consequences

- The job switch shows only the current job's instruments.
- Act is absent over the tailnet.
- The instruments group as ten under the jobs, reached by convergence; the Observatory's own records own the instruments.
