# GDR-KI-DESIGN-035: Art is unlicensed until its licensing is recorded

## Context

Who owns AI-generated art, and under which generator terms, is not yet known; the roadmap item KI-DESIGN-ART-002 carries the question. Early material also called the style "Ghibli-like", a style risk. Further isometric building images were offered for `ki-principal`'s `Pillars/Aesthetics/Isometric Tiles`. On 19 October 2026 Kit accepted the recommendations.

## Decision

**All references and generated art are treated as unlicensed until KI-DESIGN-ART-002 records the answer.**

- The style is described in our own words; the "Ghibli-like" style risk is stated plainly, and studio names stay out of prompts.
- Further reference images are filed into `Pillars/Aesthetics/Isometric Tiles` marked as references, not licensed art.

## Consequences

- Manifests of generated sets carry `"licence": "UNLICENSED"` until ART-002 is recorded.
- Who owns the art, and where the further reference images are, stay in [OPEN.md](OPEN.md).
