# Brief: the picture of Arcadia

## Goal

One painting of Arcadia, the origin island of the Knowledge Islands Realm, as if from a legendary time between times. It becomes the reference every Ancient tile is painted to: its light, palette, density and level of detail are the standard.

## What it must show

Arcadia's geography follows the model, so the picture teaches it:

| Place | What it is | How it appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Capital, on the highest ground | The island's civic heart: Library (canonical knowledge) and Council Hall (governance), drawn civic, never fortified (GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-011) | Pale limestone: a domed library and a colonnaded hall around an open square, lit from within; no walls, towers or battlements |
| The Port, on the coast | Entry and exchange in every mode | Stone quays and wooden docks with small sailing ships; a caravan road winding inland; a tall mooring mast; a customs house at the harbour gate |
| The Observatory | Insight | A domed observatory on a hill |
| The Lighthouse | Guidance | On a far headland |
| The Streams | Knowledge in motion | Rivers from the mountains to the sea, past a watermill (the Mill) and a quarter of workshops |
| The Ancient Forest | Wisdom | Enormous old trees at the island's heart, never built on |
| The Ruins | Retired knowledge, honoured | Old walls on an outer ridge |
| The edge of the known | The uncharted | A fog bank where the map ends |
| Trade routes | Collaboration | Faint sea lanes curving towards other islands |

Form: the island rises from a deep navy sea in hexagonal terraces; the cliff edges at the shoreline show the faint geometry of a hex grid. View: high three-quarter isometric, dusk. Hundreds of small warm lamp-lights in windows and along roads; soft cloud banks drifting in from one edge.

## Style

Painterly, richly detailed, calm and harmonious, in the tradition of illustrated atlases and environmental animation art. Buildings are crisp, readable silhouettes at small scale, in the manner of a classic isometric strategy game (Kit: "Age of Empires-esque"). Palette: deep ocean blues, forest and moss greens, sandstone, parchment, restrained brass gold. See `references/mood-website-dusk-a.png` and `-b.png`.

Never: people in close-up, text or lettering, creatures, crystals, magic glow, fantasy armour, neon, cyberpunk, glossy 3D.
