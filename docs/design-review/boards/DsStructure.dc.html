What it is, and is not

Where a person meets their knowledge and the work moving through it. Browser-first: one host runs the backend and owns every file, process and credential.

It does not replace agentic harnesses or agent apps (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Paperclip). They are workers inside the realm, shown by role.
