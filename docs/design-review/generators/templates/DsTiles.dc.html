The clock · @@gdr:005@@
AgreedBoth
12 frames per second
48 frame master cycle = 4 s
frame = floor(t × 12) mod 48
Each lane is a loop length that divides 48. Dots reset at frame 0 of their loop; the brass playhead is the shared frame index. Every lane realigns at the 4 s mark, so neighbouring tiles never drift. {{playNote}}
