## 2. The banner glyphs: answered

- **Status:** answered on 20 October 2026. Kris took option (b) (BANNER-GLYPHS a), recorded as [GDR-KI-DESIGN-037](../../docs/decisions/GDR-KI-DESIGN-037-the-banner-emoji-stay-the-written-notation-line-glyphs-may-be-a-rendered-form.md) and carried by KI-DESIGN-OBS-015. The question has left `docs/decisions/OPEN.md`.
- **Options:**
  - (a) Keep the emoji as the only notation.
  - (b) Keep the emoji as the standard's text notation, and add five single-colour line glyphs as an optional rendered form, drawn with the state's words beside them.
  - (c) Replace the emoji everywhere.
- **Recommendation and answer:** (b).
- **Candidates:** five line glyphs in the house style, marked proposed, are in `docs/briefs/banner-glyphs/`, with their brief in `docs/briefs/banner-glyph-candidates.md` (`992326d`). They are worker (a mallet), supervisor (head and shoulders, no face), named target (a map pin), on hold (an anchor) and finished (a pennant on a staff). Each passes `lintIcon`. They are not in `@knowledgeislands/icons`, and nothing uses them.
- **Route:** the change goes to the `ki-coordination` standard in `ki-agentic-harness` first, then the Work tab follows it; the hand-off below is being raised separately. Until the standard admits the rendered form, the glyphs stay proposed and the Work tab keeps printing the standard's glyphs unchanged, as you do now.
