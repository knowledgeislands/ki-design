Field Guide

Every mark on the map, once: what it is, what it means, and whether it is settled. Return here whenever the Voyage names a place you want to look up.

Geography

From realm to island: the shapes knowledge takes on the chart. Explained in the Voyage: The Wider Picture

  • Island

    Canonisland

    A body of knowledge with its own shores: one coherent domain, project or practice.

  • Archipelago

    Canonarchipelago

    A cluster of related islands that share waters and trade closely.

  • Territory

    Decidedterritory No glyph yet

    A region of the realm held together by purpose; its parts are mostly, though not always, adjacent.

  • Realm

    Decidedrealm No glyph yet

    The whole standard world, containing many territories laid out geographically.

  • Map of Known Lands

    Decidedmap-of-known-lands

    The chart of the realm: everything charted so far, often called simply Known Lands.

  • Terrain

    Canonterrain

    The character of the ground on a hex, from grassland to volcanic, set by the tile set.

  • Ancient forest

    Canonancient-forest

    Old, deep-rooted knowledge at the heart of an island; slow-growing and protected.

  • Weave

    Canonweave

    The fabric of cross-links that binds islands and their contents together.

  • Cartographic grid

    Decidedcartographic-grid

    The hex grid every map is drawn on; the shared coordinate system of the realm.

    Hex grid chosen for KI maps, reusing existing isometric structure art.

Zones

The places on every island. A repository's own folders draw its island. Explained in the Voyage: The Home

  • Capital

    Proposedcapital

    The seat of the realm, where the Citadel stands.

  • Pillars

    Canonpillars No glyph yet

    The foundational principles an island or the realm rests on.

  • Calendar

    Canoncalendar

    The zone that keeps time: rhythms, cadences and dated commitments.

  • Resources

    Canonresources

    Reference materials, assets and supplies an island draws on.

  • Streams

    Canonstreams

    Flows of incoming material that feed an island over time.

  • Admin / Governance

    Canonadmin-governance

    The zone for decisions, policy and stewardship of the island itself.

  • Admin / Operations

    Canonadmin-operations

    The zone for the running of the island: routines, upkeep and housekeeping.

Structures

Buildings that stand for what an island does. Explained in the Voyage: The Home

  • Citadel

    Proposedcitadel

    The fortified heart of the Capital that houses the realm’s central institutions.

    Suggested as the home of the Tower and Council Hall.

  • Library

    Canonlibrary

    Where curated, durable knowledge is kept and can be consulted.

  • Council Hall

    Decidedcouncil-hall

    Governance: where decisions are debated and made; the parliament of the realm.

  • Tower

    Proposedtower

    A place to watch, see and guide from.

    Candidate new name for the Observatory app.

  • Observatory

    Canonobservatory

    Insight and analytics: where an island looks out and reflects.

    Also the local app's working name; the app is marked by the Tower, so this keeps its canon meaning.

  • Lighthouse

    Decidedlighthouse

    Guidance: standing direction that keeps travellers off the rocks.

  • Port

    Decidedport

    Where knowledge and work arrive and depart, by whatever mode of transport.

    Replaces Harbour: a port has more modes than a harbour (dock, rail depot, airport, customs house).

  • Bridge

    Decidedbridge

    A translation layer joining two lands so that what crosses can be understood on both sides.

    Open question: whether a land-to-land link should be a Causeway, keeping Bridge for translation.

  • Workshop

    Proposedworkshop

    Where things are made and work in progress lives.

  • Mill / Engine house

    Proposedmill

    Operational systems: the machinery that keeps turning, such as automations and pipelines.

  • Ruins

    Proposedruins

    Retired knowledge: kept for history, no longer maintained or relied upon.

Signals

Marks that ask for attention. Every signal has a shape and a word, never colour alone. Explained in the Voyage: The Cycle

  • Incoming (+)

    Canonincoming

    Something has arrived and awaits attention.

  • Outbound (-)

    Canonoutbound

    Something is leaving or has been sent on.

  • Lantern

    Canonlantern

    Attention: a light left burning where someone should look.

  • Storm

    Canonstorm No glyph yet

    A blocking risk: work cannot safely proceed until it passes.

  • Whale song

    Proposedwhale-song No glyph yet

    A long-range signal heard across many islands.

  • Quiet

How the map works

Hex for logic, isometric for life.

The hex grid is the truth; the painting is how that truth is shown. Every tile is data first and scenery second.

  1. 01Hex topology

    The grid is the truth: each hex holds identity, terrain and adjacency. Routes are computed here.

  2. 02Data and layers

    Terrain, routes and structures are data on the hexes. The flat map is the Modern twin used in the app and in print.

  3. 03Isometric render

    The same data in 2:1 dimetric: plinths, height and light from the top-left. Nothing is invented.

  4. 04The map

    Structures stand on their hexes and routes join them. Either tile set can paint it without changing the data.
