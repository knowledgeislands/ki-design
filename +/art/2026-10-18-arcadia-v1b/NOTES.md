# Arcadia v1b candidates

These are the two repaints already generated in this chat, copied without alteration into the inbound area. They are candidates for review; the chosen `docs/briefs/references/arcadia.png` and the live website hero have not been replaced.

The edit target was variation 1, `docs/briefs/references/arcadia.png`, after reading `docs/briefs/arcadia-handoff.md` and `docs/briefs/arcadia-picture.md`. The prompts incorporate the owner's subsequent Capital and Port decisions. Generation used the built-in ImageGen tool. Version 1 used an initial edit followed by a refinement; version 2 was edited independently from the original. All three exact prompt texts are preserved below.

Both PNGs are 1672 × 941 pixels, the native size returned despite requests for 3840 × 2160 or at least 2048 pixels wide. Neither has been resized, upscaled, retouched or re-encoded. Treat generated art as unlicensed until the repository's art-licensing decision is recorded.

## Version 1 — arcadia-v1b-1.png

Pixel size: 1672 × 941.

Changed: stronger hex terraces, the Library-adjacent tower removed, lower surrounding ridges, clearer railway, softened quays and a calmer sunset.

Lost: some rugged cliff texture and faint sea-route markings; the atmosphere, distant islands, lighthouse and observatory are retained. The customs checkpoint remains less distinct than in version 2.

## Version 2 — arcadia-v1b-2.png

Pixel size: 1672 × 941.

Changed: broader open sea, a welcoming Capital without its tower, stepped quays, distinct inland tracks, ships and airship, and a clearer customs inspection gate.

Lost: the middle-left islet, some foreground fog and mountain drama; the distant islands and sense of exploration remain.

### Version 1 — initial edit

Input: the original `docs/briefs/references/arcadia.png`; transparent background disabled.

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
EDIT TARGET: the referenced image is the exact chosen Arcadia variation 1 painting. Produce ONE revised painting, not a collage, not a redesign, no labels or website text. Preserve the same 16:9 landscape composition, elevated three-quarter viewpoint, coastline layout, rich painted atlas/animation-art brushwork, dusk lighting, deep navy sea, forest/moss greens, warm limestone and hundreds of small inhabited amber lights. Preserve the distant islands, layered horizon and inviting sense of exploration and distance with particular fidelity. Keep the ancient central forest and great tree, streams and waterfalls, mill and workshops, outer right ruins, right observatory dome on its hill, far-left-headland lighthouse, lower coastal Port and airship, all in their existing relative positions. The lighthouse stays exactly recognisable; it is NOT the tower to remove.
Change ONLY these five things, as a restrained high-fidelity repaint:
1. Cliff terraces: at the existing shoreline and several plateau rims make connected 120-degree angles and stepped hexagonal terrace geometry clearly readable, natural weathered grey stone topped with green turf; not random polygon rocks, not a grid superimposed on the water, not giant honeycomb paving.
2. Capital: the Library dome and adjacent colonnaded Council Hall remain the civic centre in the upper middle-right. Completely REMOVE the tall narrow watchtower immediately to the RIGHT of the large Library dome. Replace its footprint with the welcoming open public square/garden shared by the Library and Hall. No keep, watchtower, battlement or fortification. Raise the Capital's existing green terraced hill enough, and gently lower the island's immediately surrounding mountain ridges if necessary, so the CAPITAL GROUND ITSELF is above ALL surrounding peaks of this main island, not merely the dome taller than peaks. Keep the same civic buildings and almost the same image positions; use the available former tower height rather than moving or scaling the whole island. Distant other islands on the horizon remain unchanged.
3. Port: soften the existing front harbour walls into low broad weathered stone quays, waterfront stairs and planted civic terraces, with no crenellations, arrow slits or defensive parapets. Retain its established lower-coast position and warm architecture.
4. Clearly separate the Port's four readable functions at thumbnail size: sailing ships tied to stone docks/wooden jetties; the existing long rail shed with a distinct PAIR OF STEEL RAILS AND REPEATED CROSS-SLEEPERS emerging from its open end and visibly curving inland to the right behind the town; the existing airship tethered at its mooring mast; and a small customs house beside the arrival route, a simple low inspection gate/crossbar across the route. Make actual railway track visible and unmistakable, not a road or decorative curved line. Keep infrastructure clear, plausible and modest.
5. The left third becomes a wider-feeling calm expanse of deep blue open sea suitable for a website headline overlay. Soften the bright orange sunset into a gentle muted warm dusk, reduce glare, wave contrast and tiny foreground boat/route-line clutter there, while keeping the original horizon, distant islands and lighthouse. Keep the small offshore islets if possible, subdued rather than prominent. No headline, no copy, no scrim painted into the artwork.
Output one highest-resolution landscape PNG available: request 3840 x 2160, or the largest supported 16:9 size, at least 2048 pixels wide if possible. No artificial border. Preserve the source painting's atmosphere and detail, not photorealism or glossy 3D. No lettering, people close-up, creatures, crystals, magic glow, neon or fantasy armour.
```

Replace that close connected ridge with substantially LOWER wooded hills that are visibly BELOW THE CAPITAL SQUARE'S GROUND LEVEL, and/or subtly lift the existing Capital hill within its existing upper-middle footprint. Preserve the existing distant islands and offshore islets and sense of distance. Sea stays richly painted but lower contrast on the left. No headline or graphic overlay in the image.
Produce ONE finished landscape painting at the largest available NATIVE image dimensions. Specifically request 3840 x 2160 pixels, or 2048 pixels wide minimum if possible, while preserving the 16:9 composition. No borders, lettering, labels, montage, close-up people, neon, crystals, magic glow or glossy 3D. The image should still be recognisably the same painting.
